## How to raise a complaint

Write to grievance@oyebuddy.in with:

1. your name and the email address on your Oye Buddy account;
2. what happened, with the profile name and date (screenshots help);
3. what you would like us to do.

You can also use **Report** in any chat for problems with another user.

## Our timelines

- We **acknowledge your complaint within 24 hours**.
- We **resolve it within 15 days** of receipt.
- Complaints about content that exposes someone's private area, shows nudity or sexual acts, or impersonates someone (including morphed images) are acted on **within 24 hours** of receipt, as required by Rule 3(2)(b) of the IT Rules 2021.

If you are not satisfied with our response to a personal-data complaint, you may approach the **Data Protection Board of India**.

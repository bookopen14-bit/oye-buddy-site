# Safety tips

Most people on OyeBuddy are here to meet someone nice nearby. These habits keep it that way.

## Before you meet

- Chat in the app first. Keep your phone number and social handles private until you trust someone.
- Video call before meeting if you can. Real people are happy to.
- Never send money, gifts, OTPs, or UPI or bank details, no matter the story.

## When you meet

- Meet in a busy public place: a café, a mall, a park in daylight.
- Tell a friend where you are going and who you are meeting. Share your live location with them, not with your date.
- Arrange your own transport there and back.
- Stay in control of your drink and your belongings.
- If something feels off, leave. You do not owe anyone an explanation.

## In the app

- Your exact location is never shown. Others see an approximate distance only, and shared pins are rounded.
- Use **Block** to stop all contact instantly. Use **Report** if someone is abusive, fake, underage, or asks for money.

## In an emergency

Call **112** (India emergency number). Women can also call the **181** women helpline. For cyber crime, call **1930** or visit cybercrime.gov.in.

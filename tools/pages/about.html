About

OyeBuddy is The Social Plans App, made in India

We are building a calmer, safer dating app for adults to meet new people nearby, make friends, find a date, and turn a plan into a real meetup across India.

What OyeBuddy does

  • Discover singles nearby, with a rounded distance in kilometres — friends, a date, or something casual.
  • Buddy Feed: post a one-line plan like "Cafe at 6? Anyone up?". It disappears on its own.
  • Buddy Like and Rewind on Discover, so you can signal interest and undo a pass.
  • Nearby: a live map of busy areas, never pins on people.
  • Chat once you both like each other.

Privacy and safety first

Your location is stored rounded to about 1 km and your exact location is never shown. OyeBuddy is for adults 18 and over only. You can report, block or unmatch anyone, our safety team reviews reports under written Community Guidelines, and you can delete your account at any time. Read our Privacy Policy, Terms and Safety tips.

Who we are

OyeBuddy is operated by Oyeteck Innovations in India. The Social Plans App is coming soon on Google Play for Android (Dating category), and it is free to use. Tell us what to fix at support@oyebuddy.in.

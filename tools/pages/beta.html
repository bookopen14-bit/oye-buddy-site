Get the OyeBuddy Android beta

OyeBuddy is a dating and friends app for meeting real people nearby. The beta is open, free, Android only, and strictly 18+.

Download for Android (APK)

Open this page in Chrome (not inside the Instagram or WhatsApp browser), or the download may not start. The file is about 172 MB, so use Wi-Fi.

Link not working? Copy this into Chrome:
https://github.com/bookopen14-bit/oye-buddy-site/releases/download/beta-v9/OyeBuddy-beta.apk

  1. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser (Settings, Install unknown apps).
  2. Open OyeBuddy and sign up with email or Google. No invite code is needed.
  3. Add at least one photo, then say oye!

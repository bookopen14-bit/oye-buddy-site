OyeBuddy support

Need help with The Social Plans App — the dating app to meet new people nearby? We are here.

Email us

Write to support@oyebuddy.in for account help, feedback, safety reports, or questions about Buddy Feed, Discover, Nearby, or chat.

  • Include the email on your OyeBuddy account when you can.
  • For safety emergencies in India, call 112.
  • For formal complaints, see the Grievance Officer.

In the app

You can block, report or unmatch from a profile or chat. Delete your account any time under You → Settings, or use Delete account on the web.

Inside the app

Made for the people around you

No endless pen-pal swiping. Oye Buddy is built around who is close by, what they feel like doing, and whether you can trust them.

Discover

Around you, one card at a time

Discover shows people near you with an approximate distance, their interests and what they are looking for. Like or pass. When you both like each other, it's a match.

  • Filters for distance, age range and who you want to see
  • A blue tick shows who has passed selfie verification
  • Free likes every day, reset at midnight IST
Buddy Feed

Post a plan. It disappears.

“Chai at 6, join me?” Buddy Feed is for short, real-time plans with people nearby. Posts expire after a few hours, so the feed is always about now.

  • Reply to a post to start a conversation
  • Heart the plans you like
  • Public places, friendly plans, nothing that sticks around
Nearby

An area map, not a tracking map

See where people are active by neighbourhood, from your area up to city and state level. Tap an area to see who is around. Nobody, including you, ever sees an exact pin.

  • Locations are stored rounded to about 1 km
  • Others see an approximate distance only
  • Pins shared in chat are rounded to about 100 m
Chats

Conversations that go somewhere

New matches at the top, conversations below, with unread badges and read receipts. Quick replies help break the ice, and push notifications tell you when someone writes back.

  • Chat once you match. Subscribers can also message first.
  • Choose which notifications you get
  • Safety tools are one tap away in every chat
Verified

The blue tick means a real person

Take one live selfie in a random pose. A trained member of our safety team compares it with your profile photos. Pass, and your profile gets the verified badge.

  • No automated face recognition, no face template
  • The selfie is deleted 30 days after the decision
  • Show only verified people, or let only verified people message you

Safety & privacy

Your comfort comes first

Safety is built into every screen, not hidden in a help centre.

Block

Stops all contact instantly. No explanation needed.

Report

Fake, abusive, underage or asking for money? Reports are confidential and reviewed by our team.

Unmatch

Changed your mind? Unmatch and the conversation ends for both of you.

Approximate location

Your location is rounded to about 1 km. People see a distance, never where you are.

Photo screening

Uploaded photos are checked for nudity. Anything flagged is hidden until our team reviews it.

Verified-only mode

Choose to see only verified people, or allow messages only from verified people.

FAQ

Questions, answered

When can I download Oye Buddy?

Oye Buddy is coming soon to Google Play for Android. Join the beta waitlist above and we'll email you as soon as you can install it.

Is Oye Buddy free?

Yes. Everyone gets a daily number of free likes, Buddy Feed hearts and replies, reset at midnight IST. Optional plans unlock extras like unlimited likes and seeing who liked you. Plans and prices are the same for everyone, and the full terms are shown in the app before any payment.

Can people see my exact location?

No. Your location is stored rounded to about 1 km, and other people only see an approximate distance. The Nearby map shows activity by area, never individual pins. If you share a pin in a chat, it is rounded to about 100 m.

How does selfie verification work?

It's optional. You take one live selfie in a random pose with your front camera. A trained member of our safety team compares it with your profile photos. We don't use automated face recognition or create a face template. The selfie is deleted 30 days after the decision, or right away if you withdraw or delete your account.

What is the Buddy Feed?

Short, public plans from people nearby, like “Chai at 6, join me?”. Posts disappear after a few hours. Reply to one to start a chat.

What if someone makes me uncomfortable?

Open the chat menu and tap Block to stop all contact, Report to tell our safety team, or Unmatch to end the conversation. Reports are confidential. For serious issues you can also contact our Grievance Officer. In an emergency, call 112.

Who can use Oye Buddy?

Adults aged 18 and over, with one account per person and real photos of themselves. See our Community Guidelines.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to You → Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate. If you can't open the app, see Delete account to request it by email.

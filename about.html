About

OyeBuddy is a dating and friends app made in Raipur

We are building a calmer, safer way for adults to meet real people nearby, starting in Raipur, Chhattisgarh.

What OyeBuddy does

  • Discover people near you, with a rounded distance in kilometres.
  • Buddy Feed: post a one-line plan like "Cafe at 6? Anyone up?". It disappears on its own.
  • Nearby: a live map of busy areas, never pins on people.
  • Chat once you both like each other.

Privacy and safety first

Your location is stored rounded to about 1 km and your exact location is never shown. OyeBuddy is for adults 18 and over only. You can report, block or unmatch anyone, our safety team reviews reports under written Community Guidelines, and you can delete your account at any time. Read our Privacy Policy, Terms and Safety tips.

Who we are

OyeBuddy is operated by Oyeteck Innovations from Raipur, India. We are in an open Android beta, Raipur first, and free during the beta. Things may be rough; tell us what to fix at support@oyebuddy.in.
